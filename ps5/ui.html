Start PSCloud with your console

PSCloud runs while its payload is loaded. To have it start by itself after every boot, add it to your autoloader:

  1. Copy pscloud-dashboard.elf from the PSCloud release to your PC or phone.
  2. In ProsperoMgr: open Payloads and upload the ELF.
  3. Open Settings → Autoloader, add PSCloud to the sequence, turn the autoloader on, and save.

Other autoloaders work the same way: point them at the PSCloud ELF. Your settings live in /data/pscloud, so they are kept across restarts. If the PS5 goes into rest mode, Activity shows how long PSCloud was paused, and a nightly backup missed during rest runs when the console is back on.

Encrypt backups in the cloud Off

New uploads are locked with your password (AES-256) before they leave this PS5, so your cloud provider cannot read them. Backups on this PS5 and on USB stay as they are.

Keep this password safe. Without it, encrypted cloud backups cannot be restored by anyone. To sync, use the same password on your other PS5.
